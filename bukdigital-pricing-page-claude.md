# BUK Digital Pricing Page --- Claude Design Brief

## Goal

Redesign `/pricing` so the page sells **services and outcomes first**,
rather than making the website feel like a price list.

The visitor should quickly understand:

-   What BUK Digital can do for their business
-   That services can be tailored to their needs
-   That they can get started from **R499/month**
-   That there is a **R5,000 setup fee**, shown clearly but kept
    visually secondary
-   The primary action is to **request a quote**, not simply choose a
    cheap package

The page should feel modern, professional, trustworthy and service-led.

## Pricing Section

Do NOT make three large pricing cards the main focus.

Instead, introduce pricing after the services:

### Simple Pricing. Built Around Your Needs.

Every business is different. Tell us what you need and we'll recommend
the right solution.

Large/highlighted text:

**From R499/month**

Supporting text:

> Get a tailored quote based on the services your business needs.

Then show the setup fee in significantly smaller text:

> **Setup fee: R5,000**\
> One-time implementation and initial setup fee. Final pricing depends
> on the scope of your project.

The R5,000 setup fee should be readable and transparent, but it should
not compete visually with the main service messaging.

## Design Direction

-   Keep the page concise.
-   Avoid excessive pricing tables.
-   Use service cards/sections instead of plan comparison cards.
-   Make the services the primary visual hierarchy.
-   Use clear icons or subtle visual elements for each service category.
-   Keep plenty of whitespace.
-   Use BUK Digital's existing brand identity and colours.
-   Keep typography clean and premium.
-   Avoid making the page look like a cheap subscription service.
-   The R499/month figure should communicate accessibility, not define
    the brand.
-   The R5,000 setup fee should appear as supporting information, not a
    headline.
-   Use strong CTA buttons throughout the page without overusing them.

## Copy Principles

Do:

-   Sell the **solution**, not the price.
-   Focus on business outcomes.
-   Make pricing transparent without making it the centrepiece.
-   Use "from R499/month" rather than presenting R499 as a fixed
    universal price.
-   Encourage visitors to request a quote.
-   Keep the language simple and confident.

Avoid:

-   "Cheap"
-   "Lowest prices"
-   "Best price"
-   Overly complicated pricing tables
-   Too many package tiers
-   Long paragraphs
-   Making R499/month look like the only option
-   Hiding the setup fee

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Important Pricing Language

Use:

> **Get quoted today from as little as R499/month.**

And beneath it in smaller text:

> **One-time setup fee: R5,000. Final pricing depends on your selected
> services and project requirements.**

Do not imply that every service or project is available for R499/month.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Primary Objective

The visitor should leave the page thinking:

**"BUK Digital can handle the digital side of my business, and I can get
a tailored quote without having to figure everything out myself."**

The page should therefore feel more like a **digital solutions
consultation page** than a traditional SaaS pricing page.
